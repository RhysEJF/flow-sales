# Licence FAQ

Plain-English answers. The licence text in `LICENSE.md` is what counts.

**We are a company with a sales team. Can we use FlowSales for free?**
Yes. Install it, run it on your own CRM data, modify it, keep your changes private. No seat limits, no time limit.

**Can our sales trainer or consultant run it for us?**
Yes. Contractors, agencies and consultants may run FlowSales on your behalf and charge you for their time. They may not resell FlowSales itself or host it for many clients as a service.

**We are a sales training consultancy. Can we use FlowSales with our clients?**
Yes, as long as each client runs FlowSales on infrastructure the client controls (their laptops, their Claude Code, their CRM). You can charge for installation, configuration, workshops and coaching around it. If you want to bundle FlowSales into a paid programme as your own product, or host it for clients, ask for a commercial licence.

**Can we build a product on top of it and sell that?**
Not without a commercial licence.

**Can we remove the logo or the "Powered by FlowSales" footer from the reports?**
No. Keeping the FlowSales name, logo and notices where FlowSales puts them is a condition of the free licence. A commercial licence can relax this.

**Can we share our fork publicly?**
Yes, free of charge, under the same licence, with the branding intact and a note that it is modified and not endorsed by FlowSales.

**Do the reports, briefings and data FlowSales produces belong to us?**
Yes. Output is yours, entirely. Sell it, share it, put it in your board deck.

**Is this open source?**
No. It is source-available. The code is public and free to use, but the licence is not an OSI-approved open source licence because it restricts paid embedding, hosting and white-labelling.

**Does any of our data leave our machine?**
FlowSales stores everything locally in a `.flow-sales/` folder. The only network calls are the ones you configure (your CRM, your Granola account) and the model calls your own Claude Code session already makes. FlowSales has no server.
